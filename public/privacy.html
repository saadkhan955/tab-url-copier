Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Tab URL Copier is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how the extension handles your data and confirms our zero-data-collection model.

1. No Data Collection

Tab URL Copier does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing histories, page contents, keystrokes, or unique user identifiers.

2. Permissions Used & Purpose

  • tabs: Used solely to read the URLs and titles of currently open tabs in your browser session so you can select and copy them. This data is processed completely in-memory on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • clipboardWrite: Used to paste the selected formatted URLs directly into your system clipboard when you click "Copy URLs".

3. Local Storage Preferences

The extension uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to save your UI preferences (such as light/dark mode selection, copy format, and domain grouping toggle). This data never leaves your computer.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

Tab URL Copier makes zero external network requests during its operation. There are no analytics libraries, tracking scripts, ad networks, or external telemetry of any kind.
