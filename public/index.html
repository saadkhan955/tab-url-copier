🔒 Private by Design • No Extension Network Requests

Everything Happens Locally in Chrome

The extension itself makes zero external network requests and does not collect or transmit your tab data. All tab URLs and titles are processed strictly in local browser memory.

● Permission: tabs

Used strictly to read active tab URLs and titles so you can select and format them. Does not record or monitor your general browsing history.

● Permission: clipboardWrite

Used strictly to write your formatted text, Markdown, HTML, or JSON links to your system clipboard when you click Copy.
